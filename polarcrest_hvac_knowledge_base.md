## SECTION 9: EMERGENCY TRIAGE PROTOCOL

### What Qualifies as an HVAC Emergency?

**TRUE EMERGENCY — Dispatch within 2–4 hours:**
1. **No heat during extreme cold** (below –10°C outside, or Environment Canada cold warning)
2. **Gas smell or suspected gas leak** → Evacuate, call Enbridge Gas at 1-866-763-5427, then (905) 712-4499. Do NOT send technician until gas company clears the site.
3. **Carbon monoxide alarm triggered** → Evacuate, call 911. Then (905) 712-4499.
4. **Furnace making loud banging/booming sounds** (possible cracked heat exchanger — carbon monoxide risk)
5. **Flooding from HVAC equipment** causing property damage
6. **No cooling during extreme heat alert** for vulnerable residents (elderly, medical needs, infants)

**URGENT (Same-day, within business hours):**
- No heat but temperature is mild (above 5°C)
- A/C not working in summer (non-extreme heat)
- Thermostat completely unresponsive
- Water dripping from equipment

**NON-URGENT (Schedule within 3–5 days):**
- Unusual noise but system still running
- Slightly uneven heating/cooling
- High energy bill inquiry
- Routine maintenance overdue

## SECTION 11: FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQs)

**Q: How often should I service my furnace?**
A: Once per year — ideally in the fall before heating season (September–October).

**Q: How often should I service my A/C?**
A: Once per year — ideally in the spring before cooling season (April–May).

**Q: How long does a furnace last?**
A: A well-maintained gas furnace lasts 15–20 years. If yours is over 15 years old and requiring frequent repairs, replacement is usually more cost-effective.

**Q: How long does a central A/C last?**
A: 12–15 years with regular maintenance.

**Q: Is the diagnostic fee waived if I proceed with a repair?**
A: Yes. The $99 diagnostic fee is applied toward the repair if you proceed on the same visit. If you decline the repair, the fee still applies.

**Q: Do your technicians call before arriving?**
A: Yes — every technician calls 20–30 minutes before arrival.

**Q: Are your technicians licensed?**
A: All PolarCrest technicians are TSSA-registered, Gas-licensed (G2 minimum), and fully insured.

**Q: Do you offer a warranty on repairs?**
A: All labour is warranted for 90 days. Parts carry the manufacturer's warranty (typically 1–5 years).

**Q: Do you offer financing?**
A: Yes. PolarCrest offers financing on equipment replacement (furnace, A/C, heat pump) with approved credit. Ask your technician for details.

**Q: Do you work on older oil furnaces?**
A: We service oil furnaces for diagnostics and minor repairs. We also offer oil-to-gas conversion services.

**Q: Can I replace just the outdoor A/C unit without replacing the indoor furnace?**
A: In some cases, yes — but a mismatched system can reduce efficiency. Our technician will assess and advise.

**Q: What brands of thermostat do you recommend?**
A: We recommend the Ecobee SmartThermostat — Canadian-made, great energy-saving features, and qualifies for rebates. Google Nest is also solid.

**Q: Do you clean dryer vents?**
A: Yes — dryer vent cleaning is $99 and highly recommended annually. Clogged dryer vents are a leading cause of house fires in Ontario.

**Q: My rental property needs HVAC service — can you invoice the landlord directly?**
A: Yes, with written authorization from the property owner. We can accommodate third-party invoicing.
