## SECTION 9: EMERGENCY TRIAGE PROTOCOL

### What Qualifies as an HVAC Emergency?

**TRUE EMERGENCY — Dispatch within 2–4 hours:**
1. **No heat during extreme cold** (Environment Canada cold warning, or below –10°C outside)
2. **Gas smell or suspected gas leak** → IMMEDIATE: Tell customer to evacuate, call Enbridge Gas at 1-877-362-7434, then 911. Do NOT send technician until gas company clears the site.
3. **Carbon monoxide alarm triggered** → IMMEDIATE: Evacuate, call 911. Do not re-enter until cleared.
4. **Furnace making loud banging/booming sounds** (possible cracked heat exchanger — carbon monoxide risk)
5. **Flooding from HVAC equipment** causing property damage
6. **No cooling during extreme heat alert** for vulnerable residents (elderly, medical needs, infants)

**URGENT (Same-day, within business hours):**
- No heat but temperature is mild (above 5°C)
- A/C not working in summer (non-extreme heat)
- Thermostat completely unresponsive
- Water dripping from equipment

**NON-URGENT (Schedule within 3–5 days):**
- Unusual noise but system still running
- Slightly uneven heating/cooling
- High energy bill inquiry
- Routine maintenance overdue

### Emergency Triage Script for AI:
1. "Is anyone in the home or building experiencing difficulty breathing, dizziness, or nausea?" → If yes, advise 911 immediately.
2. "Do you smell gas?" → If yes, follow gas leak protocol above.
3. "Is the carbon monoxide detector alarming?" → If yes, evacuate + 911.
4. "What is the current indoor temperature, and what is it outside?" → Assess heat/cold risk.
5. If true emergency confirmed: "I'm flagging this as an emergency right now. A technician will contact you within 30 minutes to confirm ETA. Please keep your phone nearby."

## SECTION 11: FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQs)

**Q: How often should I service my furnace?**
A: Once per year — ideally in the fall before heating season (September–October). Annual tune-ups improve efficiency, extend equipment life, and are often required to keep manufacturer warranties valid.

**Q: How often should I service my A/C?**
A: Once per year — ideally in the spring before cooling season (April–May).

**Q: How long does a furnace last?**
A: A well-maintained gas furnace lasts 15–20 years. If yours is over 15 years old and requiring frequent repairs, replacement is usually more cost-effective.

**Q: How long does a central A/C last?**
A: 12–15 years with regular maintenance.

**Q: My furnace is running but the house isn't heating. What should I check before calling?**
A: Check the thermostat is set to HEAT and the temperature is set higher than the current room temp. Check your furnace filter — a clogged filter can cause the furnace to shut down. Check that all vents are open and unobstructed. If the furnace still won't produce heat, call us.

**Q: Do you offer financing?**
A: Yes. NorthFlow offers financing on equipment replacement (furnace, A/C, heat pump) through our lending partner, with approved credit. Terms from 6 months no-interest to 10-year fixed rate plans. Ask your technician or our team for details.

**Q: Is the diagnostic fee waived if I proceed with a repair?**
A: The $119 diagnostic fee covers the call-out and first 30 minutes of labour. If you proceed with the repair on the same visit, it is applied toward the total invoice. If you decline the repair, the diagnostic fee still applies.

**Q: Do your technicians call before arriving?**
A: Yes — every technician calls or texts 20–30 minutes before arrival.

**Q: Are your technicians licensed?**
A: All NorthFlow technicians are TSSA-registered, Gas-licensed (G2 minimum), and fully insured. We perform background checks on all field staff.

**Q: Do you offer a warranty on repairs?**
A: Yes. All labour is warranted for 90 days. Parts carry the manufacturer's warranty (typically 1–5 years depending on brand and component).

**Q: My rental property needs HVAC service — can you invoice the landlord directly?**
A: Yes, with written authorization from the property owner. We can accommodate split billing or third-party invoicing for property management clients.

**Q: Do you work on older oil furnaces?**
A: We service oil furnaces for diagnostics and minor repairs. We also offer oil-to-gas conversion services if you're interested in switching to natural gas, which is typically more cost-effective in the GTA.

**Q: Can I replace just the outdoor A/C unit without replacing the indoor furnace?**
A: In some cases, yes — but a mismatched system can reduce efficiency and cause compatibility issues. Our technician will assess your existing indoor coil and advise whether a full matched system replacement makes more financial sense.

**Q: What brands of thermostat do you recommend?**
A: For most residential clients, we recommend the Ecobee SmartThermostat — it's Canadian-made, has excellent energy-saving features, and qualifies for rebates. Google Nest is also a solid option.

**Q: Do you clean dryer vents?**
A: Yes — dryer vent cleaning is $99 and highly recommended annually. Clogged dryer vents are a leading cause of house fires in Ontario.

- Collect name, address, phone, and email before confirming any booking
